Wilton Flag Rules: K, 1st, 2nd grade

5-on-5 NFL Flag with league tweaks. Condensed from wiltonyouthfootball.com; the site is the final word if a ref disagrees.

The five that decide games at this age
  • The QB cannot run past the line with the ball. He can only throw or hand off. The kid who takes the handoff can run or throw.
  • Nobody rushes the QB. Defenders line up 7 yards back and can only come once a handoff happens.
  • No pitches or tosses. Every handoff must be hand to hand. A forward shovel pass is fine.
  • 4 downs to cross midfield, then 3 downs to score. Start every drive at your own 5.
  • No blocking, screening, or running next to the ball carrier. Once he crosses the line, teammates near him stop moving.

Game and field

  • 30 x 40 yard field. 1st and 2nd grade use the blue junior NFL Flag ball.
  • Two 20-minute halves, 5-minute halftime.
  • Coin toss: away team calls it. Winner picks offense or defense, loser picks direction. Team that started on defense gets the ball to open the second half.
  • Three 30-second timeouts per team per game.
  • Two coaches on the field for offense, one for defense.
  • 4 vs 4 counts as a real game if a team only has four.

Equipment

  • Official NFL Flag belts. Belt color can't match or resemble the shorts.
  • Flags sit on the hips, poppers facing down and out, no belt strap hanging.
  • Cleats required, never metal. Gloves, elbow and knee pads, and soft-shell helmets are OK.
  • Jerseys tucked in. No jewelry. Hard-brim hats backwards only, beanies fine.
  • Refs check equipment at midfield before the game.

Scoring

  • Touchdown = 7 points. No extra point attempts in these divisions.
  • Safety = 2 points (ball carrier down in his own end zone, including a bad snap that lands in or past the end zone).

Offense

  • 25 seconds to snap once the ball is spotted. One warning, then delay of game.
  • At least one player (the center) on the line, up to four. QB must be off the line.
  • No "no-run zones" in these divisions. Running is allowed anywhere.
  • Ball is spotted where the ball is when the flag gets pulled, not where the feet are.
  • QB has 7 seconds to throw.
  • All passes must reach or cross the line of scrimmage.
  • Center can snap from the side or between the legs.
  • 4th down: "punt" and the other team starts at their 5; go for it and fail and they take over at the spot.
  • Once the ball is past the line, only that ball carrier can have it until the whistle.
  • Fumbled center-QB exchange: play is dead, re-snap, no lost down.
  • No jumping, leaping, or hurdling. Play is dead where the runner left the ground.

Defense and rushing

  • No rushing the QB, ever. Rushers wait until a handoff.
  • Everyone lines up 7 yards off the line. Anyone can come after the handoff, no hand-raising needed.
  • Jump the snap and that player can't rush on that play.
  • Inside the 5, defenders can line up on the goal line instead of 7 yards back.

Flag pulling

  • Only a legal pull when the runner has full possession.
  • Defenders can dive for flags but can't tackle, hold, or run through the runner.
  • Never strip or grab the ball.
  • Flag falls off on its own: runner is down where the flag lands.
  • A player with one or zero flags who gets the ball is down on the spot.
  • No pulling flags off a player who doesn't have the ball.
  • Flag guarding (stiff arm, dropping a hand, arm, shoulder or head to cover flags, tucking jersey over flags) is a penalty.

Overtime

  • Coin flip, home team calls. Winner picks first or second. First team goes for 1 from the 5 or 2 from the 10.
  • Each team gets one shot. Still tied: repeat, with the loser of the first flip choosing order.
  • Third OT: each team starts at its own 5, most yards gained wins.

Conduct

  • Intentional tackling, elbowing, cheap shots, blocking, or any unsportsmanlike act: game stops, player may be ejected, no appeals.
  • No offensive or confrontational language from anyone, including coaches.
  • Runners must try to avoid defenders who have position. Defenders must give receivers a free release off the line.
  • Unsportsmanlike penalty: defense gives 10 yards and a first down; offense loses 10 and a down. Two on the same person = out of the game.
  • League goal is safe and fun. Reckless or intentional contact gets reported to the board.
